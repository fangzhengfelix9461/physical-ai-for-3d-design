1. Physical AI for 3D design

面向三维设计的物理人工智能

I’ll start with the research question behind this proposal: how can we tell whether a generated CAD model will work in use?

我先从这份研究计划的出发点讲起。给定一个生成的CAD模型，我们怎样判断它在实际使用中能不能满足要求？

Natural-language-driven computer-aided design (CAD) generation enables users to obtain editable three-dimensional (3D) models from text. Whether these designs meet their requirements in use, however, also depends on materials, component connections, and loading conditions.

自然语言驱动的计算机辅助设计，也就是CAD生成，让用户可以通过文本获得可修改的三维模型。然而，这些设计在使用中能否满足要求，还取决于材料、部件连接和载荷条件。

Physics simulation can compute structural responses under these conditions and guide design revisions, but improving a particular design does not establish that the model has acquired capabilities that transfer to subsequent tasks. To investigate whether models can learn transferable physical relationships from simulation feedback, this research proposes three complementary studies on the evaluation, learning, and continual generalization of physical understanding.

物理仿真可以计算这些条件下的结构响应，并指导设计修改；但改进某一个设计，并不能证明模型已经获得能够迁移到后续任务的能力。为研究模型能否从仿真反馈中学到可迁移的物理关系，本研究提出三项互补研究，分别讨论物理理解的评估、学习与持续泛化。

2. From geometry to physical response

从几何形状到物理响应

To see where this question comes from, let’s look at what our earlier work on MUSE already allows us to check.

这个问题是怎么来的？先看我们之前参与的MUSE研究，以及它已经能帮我们检查什么。

MUSE combines structured design requirements with multi-component CAD models and evaluates code execution, geometric validity, and design-intent alignment through a staged protocol (Dong et al., 2026). Our work on component correspondence, interface localization, and local fit-clearance measurement within this project further links design requirements to specific components and connection locations.

MUSE将结构化设计要求与多部件CAD模型结合，通过分阶段流程评估代码执行、几何有效性以及设计意图的一致性（Dong 等，2026）。我们在该项目中开展的部件对应、接口定位与局部配合间隙测量工作，进一步将设计要求与具体部件和连接位置联系起来。

These geometric correspondences provide a basis for assigning material properties to the appropriate components and locating connection and contact conditions. Geometry locates components and interfaces; materials and connections determine how they deform and transfer loads; loads and supports define external actions and restrictions on motion.

这些几何对应关系，为将材料属性分配给相应部件，以及定位连接和接触条件提供了基础。几何用于定位部件和接口；材料与连接决定它们如何变形和传递载荷；载荷与支撑则定义外部作用和对运动的约束。

3. Related work and research position

已有工作与本研究的切入点

Turning to related work, three studies on this slide show how models can predict physical responses, generate CAD, and retain what they have learned.

接下来进入相关研究。这页的三项工作分别回答三个问题：模型怎样预测物理响应，怎样生成CAD模型，以及怎样保留已经学到的能力。

PhysWorld uses simulation-generated data to train predictors for deformable objects, showing how simulation experience can support physical learning (Yang et al., 2025). CAD-Coder provides an example of post-training for executable CAD generation. Geometric rewards alone, however, do not express loads, connections, or allowable deformation.

PhysWorld用仿真生成的数据训练模型，预测可变形物体的行为，说明模型可以从仿真经验中学习物理关系。CAD-Coder展示了如何通过后训练生成可执行的CAD程序。不过，只用几何奖励，还无法表达载荷、连接或允许的变形。

Learning from new experience also requires retaining earlier skills. Replay-TS addresses this by storing and replaying data from earlier physical conditions. These training methods also require a choice of which experience to collect.

模型学习新经验时，还要保留原有能力。Replay-TS的做法是保存并回放以往物理条件下的数据。这也引出了一个问题：接下来应该让模型收集哪些经验？

4. Research questions and hypotheses

三个研究问题与假设

I now introduce three research questions, covering evaluation, transferable design ability, and experiment selection.

现在介绍三个研究问题，分别涉及评估、可迁移的设计能力，以及实验选择。

RQ1: How can we evaluate a model’s understanding of a design’s physical behavior? The same compliant design may result from accurate prediction or extensive trial and error. RQ2: How can simulation feedback become transferable design ability?

研究问题一：如何评估模型对设计物理行为的理解？同样一个达标设计，既可能来自准确预测，也可能来自大量试错。研究问题二：仿真反馈如何转化为可迁移的设计能力？

RQ3: How can a model keep selecting useful physical experiments? Exhaustively simulating every combination of geometry, material, and connection is impractical.

研究问题三：模型如何持续选择有用的物理实验？穷尽仿真几何、材料与连接的所有组合并不现实。

5. Research plan

三项研究怎样衔接

In the research plan, I explain how the three studies will support one another.

在研究计划部分，我会说明三项研究将如何相互衔接和支持。

Study 1 constructs controlled physical tasks, records predictions before feedback, and keeps related design variants in the same data split. Study 2 uses checked simulation responses and edit outcomes for supervised fine-tuning and reward-based training.

研究一构建受控物理任务，记录反馈前的预测，并将相关设计变体保留在同一个数据划分中。研究二使用经过检查的仿真响应与修改结果，进行监督微调和基于奖励的训练。

Study 3 selects new training experiments, combines their records with earlier experience, and updates the model. Independent tests assess prediction, design success, transfer, retention, and cost without guiding learning.

研究三选择新的训练实验，将实验记录与早期经验结合，并更新模型。独立测试评估预测、设计成功、迁移、能力保持及成本，但不用于指导学习。

6. Tasks and benchmark construction

研究一怎样构造任务与基准

For Study 1, I first describe the benchmark tasks and their initial physical scope.

先看研究一：我们给模型什么任务，初期评估哪些物理现象？

Each task provides an initial model, material and connection parameters, loads and supports, requirements for intended use, editable parameters, and a computational budget. The initial benchmark will cover stability, elastic deformation, and connection behavior under static and quasistatic conditions. To test whether a model uses physical conditions, we will vary material, connection state, or loading position around the same initial design and record all changed parameters.

每项任务提供初始模型、材料和连接参数、载荷与支撑、预定用途的要求、可修改参数，以及计算预算。初始基准将覆盖静态和准静态条件下的稳定性、弹性变形与连接行为。为检验模型是否使用这些物理条件，我们会针对同一个初始设计，改变材料、连接状态或加载位置，并记录所有改变的参数。

Training and test splits will group each initial design with its close variants. Evaluation will distinguish new instances of familiar categories, unseen combinations of conditions, and unseen structures.

训练与测试划分会将每个初始设计及其相近变体归为一组。评估将区分熟悉类别的新实例、未见的条件组合，以及未见的结构。

7. Evaluation and experiments

研究一怎样评估预测和修改

For Study 1’s evaluation, I define the physical compliance rate, or PCR, as the proportion of all assigned tasks whose designs pass every applicable check.

任务确定以后，怎样判断设计是否达标？这里用到一个指标，叫物理达标率，简称PCR。它是指在全部分配的任务中，有多少比例的设计通过了每一项适用检查。

Each task first requires the model to submit and lock its response prediction before seeing the current simulation result. Feedback is then revealed, and the model may edit the design within a fixed budget. Finally, independent checks evaluate the revised geometry and physical performance.

每项任务首先要求模型在看到当前仿真结果前，提交并锁定响应预测。随后提供反馈，模型可以在固定预算内修改设计。最后，通过独立检查评估修改后的几何与物理性能。

An incorrect prediction does not prevent editing, allowing us to observe whether feedback helps the model correct its error. E1b tests whether feedback improves design.

即使预测有误，模型仍然可以修改设计，这样我们才能观察反馈是否帮助它纠正错误。实验E1b将检验反馈能否改善设计。

8. Dynamic loading

后续扩展中的冲击位置

Before moving to learning, I outline dynamic loading as an optional extension of Study 1.

在转向学习方法之前，我先介绍研究一的一项可选扩展：动态载荷。

Dynamic loading is a later extension and requires additional checks of impact conditions, contact models, and time steps. Figure 7 isolates a change in impact location while retaining the same structure, ball, and drop height.

动态载荷属于后续扩展，需要额外检查冲击条件、接触模型和时间步长。图七保持结构、球和下落高度相同，只改变冲击位置。

This contrast motivates predicting where a response occurs, rather than reporting only its maximum magnitude. It is not a result from the initial static benchmark.

这一对比说明，需要预测响应发生在哪里，而不只是报告其最大幅值。这并不是初始静态基准的实验结果。

9. Simulation data and post-training

研究二怎样从仿真反馈中学习

Study 2 asks whether what a model learns from simulation feedback on one design can help it with the next design.

接下来进入研究二。模型在一个设计上得到的仿真反馈，能不能帮助它处理下一个设计？

Data generation will use two kinds of comparison. The first varies physical conditions for the same structure and records the resulting responses. The second tries different edits to the same unsuccessful design and records responses and verification outcomes before and after each edit.

生成训练数据时，我们会做两类对比。第一种对比改变同一结构的物理条件，并记录相应响应。第二种对比针对同一个未达标设计尝试不同修改，记录每次修改前后的响应与验证结果。

Training will begin with supervised fine-tuning on response predictions with explicit units and executable edits, followed by post-training with rewards from the sandbox’s physical evaluation. Starting from the same base model, we will compare no post-training, supervised fine-tuning alone, and training with physical rewards. We will first evaluate predictions without feedback from the current simulation, then evaluate edits under equal feedback access and budgets.

训练首先通过具有明确单位的响应预测与可执行修改进行监督微调，然后利用沙盒物理评估提供的奖励进行后训练。从相同基础模型出发，我们将比较不进行后训练、仅进行监督微调，以及使用物理奖励的训练。我们将先在没有当前仿真反馈的条件下评估预测，再在反馈访问条件和预算相同的情况下评估修改。

10. Autonomous experiment selection and continual updates

研究三怎样选实验并保留已学能力

Study 3 asks which experiment the model should run next, and how it can learn from the result while retaining earlier skills.

到了研究三，我们要决定下一次仿真该做什么实验，也要看模型怎样在学习新结果的同时保留原有能力。

Fixed sampling may repeatedly generate tasks that the model has already mastered while missing structures and condition combinations on which it still makes errors. The system will therefore identify errors in training records, propose further simulation experiments, and use valid results for continued post-training. This creates a repeated cycle of selecting experiments, obtaining feedback, and updating the model.

固定采样可能反复产生模型已经掌握的任务，却遗漏它仍然出错的结构和条件组合。因此，系统会分析训练记录中的错误，提出后续仿真实验，再利用有效结果继续后训练。这样就形成了选择实验、获取反馈、更新模型的循环。

Each training round will combine new records with a limited store of earlier experience to balance adaptation and retention. At equal budgets, the study will compare gains on new tasks with losses on earlier tasks, and separately report performance on unseen structures and condition combinations.

每轮训练都会结合新记录和有限存储的早期经验，兼顾对新任务的适应与已有能力的保持。在相同预算下，我们会比较新任务上的收益与早期任务上的损失，并分别报告未见结构和条件组合上的表现。

11. Expected outcomes and potential contributions

三项研究的预期贡献

With the three studies in place, I’ll turn to what we expect them to produce and how we will evaluate those contributions.

前面讲了三项研究准备怎么做。接下来看它们预期能交付什么，以及我们将怎样检验这些贡献。

We will link changes in physical conditions to structural responses and design revision outcomes, using these records as supervision and rewards. Tests on unseen structures and combinations of conditions will examine which feedback and training objectives improve prediction and design revision on subsequent tasks.

我们会把物理条件的变化与结构响应、设计修改结果联系起来，将这些记录用作监督信号和奖励。通过未见结构和条件组合上的测试，考察哪些反馈和训练目标能改善后续任务的预测与设计修改。

We will organize task specifications, code, and experimental records for sharing, replication, and comparison. These potential contributions will be assessed on independent tests; the proposed methods are not assumed to outperform the baselines.

我们将整理任务规范、代码与实验记录，用于共享、复现和比较。这些潜在贡献将通过独立测试评估；我们并不预设所提出的方法优于基线。

12. Research schedule and milestones

三年研究计划与里程碑

Finally, I present the schedule and review points for carrying out the three studies.

最后，我会介绍开展这三项研究的时间安排与阶段评审节点。

The project will run from September 2027 to August 2030. Reviews in February 2028, February 2029, and December 2029 will assess simulation cost, training gains, and continual-learning progress, respectively.

项目将从2027年9月持续到2030年8月。2028年2月、2029年2月和2029年12月的阶段评审，将分别评估仿真成本、训练收益和持续学习进展。

The overall aim is to enable 3D design models to learn relationships between structures and physical responses from simulation, and to use them in new designs. This research investigates whether models can predict the effects of changes in these conditions and learn transferable design knowledge from simulation feedback.

总体目标是让三维设计模型从仿真中学到结构与物理响应之间的关系，并将这些关系用于新的设计。本研究要检验的是：模型能否预测物理条件变化的影响，并从仿真反馈中学到可迁移的设计知识。
